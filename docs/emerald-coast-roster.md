# Emerald Coast (Australia) — Verified Reserve Roster

*Companion data to `yukon-valley-chart.html` and the other reserve rosters. Same schema, ready to drop in. Verified against the COTW Wiki, the thehuntercotw structured stat sheet, and community fur/location data. Anything not officially published is flagged, not guessed.*

## Reserve meta

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Emerald Coast |
| Setting | Australia — outback, subtropical bushland, tropical rainforest & mangrove coast |
| Area | 64 km² / 25 mi² |
| Wardens | Robert "Robbo" Raines & Sophia "Soph" Nilsson |
| Species | 14 (incl. 2 birds + 1 reptile); 8 unique to this map |
| Great Ones | 3 — **Fallow Deer, Red Deer, Red Fox** |
| Reserve weapon | Zagan Varminter .22-250 (awarded early in the story; good for class 2–4) |
| Missions | 8 story, 9 side (no boss animal) |
| Note | 2nd COTW map with a Saltwater Crocodile; campaign themed around invasive-species conservation |

## Great Ones

**Fallow Deer** — Class 4 · Level 10 Fabled
- Max-level-5: shoot level 3–4 bucks, leave 1–2, stack the 5s. G1 reaches 140 kg / 308 lbs. Emerald Coast is one of the two maps this GO was introduced on (June 2023).
- Fabled coats: Golden, Hooded, Mocha, Painted, Silver

**Red Deer** — Class 6 · Level 10 Fabled
- Max-level-9: never shoot levels 1–4, stack the 9s. G1 reaches 260 kg / 573 lbs.
- Fabled coats: Spotted *(Red Deer Great Ones classically appear only in Fabled Spotted; the Dec 2025 remodel may have expanded this — verify in-game if it matters).*

**Red Fox** — Class 2 · Level 10 Fabled
- Max-level-9: never shoot levels 1–4, stack the 9s. Jackrabbit predator caller keeps them coming.
- Fabled coats: Blood Moon, Candycane, Cherry Blossom, Licorice, Midnight Poppy, Mystic Snowdrop, Peppermint, Rosebud Frost, Scarlet Nightshade

*Great One spawn odds are unofficial community estimates — treat it as a numbers game.*

## Accuracy notes
- Classes, weights, medal scores, zone times, callers and coats are from the thehuntercotw structured sheet, cross-checked against the COTW Wiki.
- **Weights are max only** (spotting value) — written as "up to X," no invented minimums.
- **Trophy types worth noting:** Eastern Grey Kangaroo and Saltwater Crocodile are scored by **Length** (not weight/antlers); Feral Goat and Banteng by **Horns**; Feral Pig by **Tusks**; Stubble Quail must be shot in flight.
- **Hazards:** the Saltwater Crocodile is a genuine threat (attacks, hard to kill). Feral Pig can charge. Banteng are wild cattle in large herds — treated as non-aggressive here since COTW doesn't flag them as attackers, but give big bulls room.
- **Hot spots** are biome-level (outback / bushland / rainforest / mangrove) plus the player-known Javan Rusa tea-plantation spot — reliable as areas, not pixel-exact.
- Great One spawn odds are unofficial community estimates.
